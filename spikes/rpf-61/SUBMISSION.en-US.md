## Detailed proposal

When agents change business state, developers need evidence of the final outcome. A timeout may follow a committed write, and a model's completion message may not establish receipt uniqueness or the expected version. This project serves agent developers and reliability teams working in controlled development and review environments. It extends the existing RunProof Agent Reliability & Release Engineering platform; it does not connect to production payment or configuration credentials.

Execution, Effect and Experiment contracts separately bind visible tool execution, business invariants, and experiment candidates, faults and budgets. A real DeepSeek Explorer can only select_experiment from two fixed candidates: a normal order refund or a planned-but-inhibited configuration experiment. It cannot change the oracle, source identity, budget, executable or endpoint. One durable ledger reserves resources before I/O. Repeated choices remain legal with separate run identities. Unknown outcomes retain reservations and stop further writes, permitting only appropriate read-only reconciliation. Cancellation takes effect at a safe boundary; refresh and restart cannot restore quota or resume execution.

The order domain uses a fresh SQLite database, a fictional 2,500-minor-unit full refund and a unique receipt. A separate read-only Reader and deterministic oracle inspect the business state. The configuration domain uses a pinned real SDK with a scripted Target and fresh Java17/H2. Inspect, create-v1 and update-v2 operations are checked through same-service GET readback of version 2 and history [2,1]. The domains share orchestration and accounting, while retaining different authorities. Same-service HTTP is not database-external independent authentication. Fault planned, triggered and observed are separate facts.

The bilingual desktop interface exposes provenance, lifecycle, Timeline, State Diff and event links. A browser confirmation starts the bounded session through a Vite server proxy and loopback authenticated Gateway. In the recorded RPF-60 session, the Explorer selected order then configuration. Two real Provider calls used 1,340 tokens, with a conservative estimated cost upper bound of CNY0.007191. This is a run-specific estimate, not an invoice or product price. Both domain contracts were satisfied; the configuration fault remained inhibited and no business violation was found. Verified first-round feedback preceded the second input freeze. The changed choice is observational evidence, without evidence that feedback caused it.

The witness binds selections, feedback, domain evidence, fault flags, resource accounting and source limits. A deterministic offline verifier checks content and contract consistency without model calls or business re-execution. Missing or incorrect external anchors are rejected, and rehashed semantic tampering is checked. A separate browser SHA check verifies downloaded bytes. Public fixed-commit evidence links support review; hashes do not provide issuer signatures or independent reproduction.

The separate RPF-57 configuration study used a real Explorer and real Target, observed local tool-result delivery suppression followed by read-only reconciliation, and recorded new verified facts. It is not part of the RPF-60 session. Neither study establishes causal or statistical improvement. JiuwenSwarm/WorkSwarm integration, two real LLM Targets, database-external authority, independent reproduction and production readiness remain unestablished. Future work requires authorized real-Target coverage and fair controls. Potential commercial use is agent quality review and fault investigation; willingness to pay, customer benefit and scale performance require validation.
